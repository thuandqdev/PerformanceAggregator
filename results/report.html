Methodology

  • CTR = total clicks ÷ total impressions, computed from each campaign's totals (not an average of row-level rates). Campaigns with 0 impressions have no CTR and are excluded from the CTR ranking.
  • CPA = total spend ÷ total conversions. Campaigns with 0 conversions have no CPA and are excluded from the CPA ranking.
  • Ranking uses exact values before rounding; ties are broken by campaign ID in ascending order.
  • Rounding is half up. This page shows CTR as a percentage and Spend and CPA in dollars; the ranked metric carries 4 decimals so close rows stay distinguishable, and hovering any number shows the exact CSV value.
  • The CSV files keep the challenge's format: CTR as a ratio with 4 decimals, Spend and CPA with 2.
